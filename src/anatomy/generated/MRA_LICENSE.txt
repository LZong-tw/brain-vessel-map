TopCoW MRA-derived communicating vessel shapes

The files mraCommunicatingPaths.json and mraCommunicatingAudit.json contain
source data and modified data from:
Musio et al., "Circle of Willis Centerline Graphs: A Dataset and Baseline
Algorithm" (2025 preprint, arXiv:2510.13720), TopCoW centerline dataset
released 2025-10-15, MRA case 008.
Source: https://zenodo.org/records/17358162

Changes: extracted the unique right/left PCom and right-to-left ACom paths;
omitted the ACom third-A2 side branch; applied a separate proper rotation,
translation and uniform scale to each curve for model endpoint placement.
Placement is unvalidated. No endorsement by the source authors is implied.
Original graph and node descriptions are retained in mraCommunicatingAudit.json.

Included tuberothalamic render connectors remain project-authored geometry;
only their initial attachment point follows the derived PCom curve. They are
not source-measured vessel shapes.
