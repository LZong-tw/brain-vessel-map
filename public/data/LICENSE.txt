Changes: resampled/registered to MNI152NLin2009cAsym, surfaces extracted (marching cubes),
decimated and smoothed; labels combined into functional regions x arterial territories;
vessel centrelines snapped to the vessel atlas and draped on the brain surface.

Slice data: T1 intensities quantized for display; Liu territory labels sampled with the
project's approximate atlas-to-template transform; model perfusion-bed labels retained for
fraction heatmaps. Territory outlines are reference anatomy, not simulated lesion boundaries.
